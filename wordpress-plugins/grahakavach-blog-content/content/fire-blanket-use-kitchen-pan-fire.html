A fire blanket is one of the simplest and most effective tools against a small kitchen fire. It does not need training, power or refilling. It works by covering the fire and cutting off its oxygen. The trick is to use it correctly so that you do not burn your hands or fan the flames.

When to use a fire blanket

  • A pan of oil, ghee or food that has caught fire on the stove.
  • A small fire in a bin, a toaster or a small appliance, once the power is off.
  • Clothing on fire (see the note at the end).

It is not meant for large fires, a gas cylinder fire or a fire that has already spread to cabinets or the ceiling. In those cases leave and call 101.

Step by step: a burning pan

  1. Stay calm and keep your face back. Do not lean over the pan.
  2. Switch off the heat if you can reach the knob safely. Never move the pan.
  3. Take the blanket out. Pull both tapes downwards to slide the blanket from its pouch. Keep the tapes in your hands.
  4. Wrap the top corners over your hands. Fold the upper edge of the blanket back over your fists, so your hands are protected like in a glove.
  5. Hold it in front of you as a shield, from chest to knees, and walk to the pan.
  6. Lay it over the pan gently, away from you, from the near side to the far side. Do not throw it and do not flap it.
  7. Make sure the pan is fully covered with no gaps along the edges.
  8. Leave it in place. Do not lift it for at least 15 to 20 minutes. Oil stays hot enough to catch again.
  9. Once everything is cool, remove the blanket carefully.

Never do this

  • Never pour water on the pan or on the blanket. See why water must never be used on oil fires.
  • Never lift the blanket to see if the fire is out.
  • Never carry the covered pan outside.
  • Never use a damaged, torn or contaminated blanket.

If clothing catches fire

Tell the person to stop, drop and roll, not run. If a blanket is nearby, wrap it around them from the shoulders, avoiding the face, and lay them on the ground. Call 112 immediately. Do not remove clothing stuck to the skin.

Where to keep it

Mount the blanket on a wall just outside the kitchen, near the door and at chest height. If it is hidden in a drawer or behind the stove, you will not reach it in time.
