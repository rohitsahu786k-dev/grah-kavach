Most fires do not wait until someone is home. A short circuit in a closed room at 3 a.m., a charger left on a bed, a stove left on while the family is out: in each case nobody is there to reach for an extinguisher. An automatic fire extinguisher ball is designed for exactly this gap.

What is an automatic fire ball?

It is a hollow ball filled with dry extinguishing powder. The Graha Kavach Fire Ball uses MAP powder (mono ammonium phosphate), the same family of powder found in ABC extinguishers. It weighs about 1.3 kg and comes with a stand, so it can sit on a shelf or be placed on the floor without rolling.

How it works

  1. The ball's outer shell is designed to react to flames. When fire reaches it, the shell breaks open.
  2. The powder is released and spreads over the surrounding area.
  3. The powder interrupts the chemical reaction of the fire and smothers it, helping to put out or hold back a small fire.

You do not have to pull a pin, press a lever or even be present. It can also be thrown into a small fire by hand if you are there.

Where it helps most

  • Unoccupied rooms: storerooms, guest rooms, the area near the electrical panel.
  • Night-time risk: rooms with chargers, inverters, heaters or an AC.
  • Shops and godowns: places that stay closed for hours. See our small shop fire safety checklist.
  • Kitchen entrance: near, but not directly above, the cooking area.

Where not to put it

  • Directly over a stove or burner, where everyday heat could trigger it.
  • Where it could be knocked or stepped on, or reached by small children and pets.
  • Outdoors or anywhere exposed to rain and strong sun.

Honest limits

A fire ball is a helpful extra layer, not a replacement for an extinguisher or blanket.

  • It works best on small fires in a contained area. A large or fast-spreading fire will overwhelm it.
  • It covers a limited area. Keep one in each high-risk room rather than assuming one ball protects the entire house.
  • It does not warn anyone. Pair it with a smoke alarm so people wake up and get out.
  • Once it has gone off, it is used up and must be replaced.

How long does it last?

The brochure-listed shelf life is five years. Write the installation date on the stand or in your calendar, and check the shell monthly for cracks, dents or discolouration. Replace it if damaged.

Fire ball, extinguisher and blanket together

Each tool covers a different moment. The fire ball works while you are away, the extinguisher gives you control when you are present, and the fire blanket smothers a pan or clothing fire instantly.
